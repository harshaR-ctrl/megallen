      How to use this console

      What am I looking at?

      A detector that checks network connections one by one and tells you whether each looks normal, like a known attack, or like an attack it has never seen (a possible zero-day). Every alert explains itself in plain words.

      Where does the data come from?

      Try it in three steps

      1. Play test traffic (top bar). Recorded connections stream in and get checked.
      2. Send zero-day attack (Live monitor). Sends attacks of the type the model never saw in training.
      3. Open an alert (Alerts tab). Read "In short", then the checks below it.

      What the verdicts mean

      Nothing here watches a real network. Your reviews are saved to an audit log and never change the model.
